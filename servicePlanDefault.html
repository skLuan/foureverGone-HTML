Treatment Overview

The first two rounds in the spring will involve a proactive approach at stopping weeds, grubs and other diseases from infecting your lawn. A healthy, green lawn is the best defense against most lawn issues, so we fertilize your lawn on each treatment.

The second two rounds, completed in the summer, will be focused on building on what we've already done to keep your pests and lawn under control and focus on keeping your lawn green! We will continue to treat for weeds as needed, but it should not be necessary! This is also when we apply typically apply our grub treatment. 

The last two rounds will be done in the fall and will once again consist of a blanket treatment of weed control to prevent any of the late blooming weeds before they deposit their seeds. This will be most beneficial for the following spring, where there will be much less weed pressure in your lawn at the beginning of the following year!
100% SATISFACTION GUARANTEED! Over time weed levels will continually decrease as regular services are performed. Each round of treatments is critical as each has a different vital role to play in maintaining a healthy lawn, stopping weed establishment, and maintaining your warranty. If you see more than the occasional weed on your lawn, please call {{officePhone}} at any time for a complimentary retreat!
